My Plants 🌱

A SwiftUI iOS app that helps you keep track of your plants and remember to water them. Add a reminder for each plant, tick it off when you've taken care of it, and get a notification if you forget.

Features
Plant reminders: add a plant with its name, room, light level, watering schedule, and water amount.
Edit: tap any plant to change its details.
Delete: swipe a plant to the left, or open it and tap Delete Reminder.
Daily progress: tick a plant when it's watered; a progress bar shows how many plants feel loved today.
All Done screen: shown when every plant has been taken care of.
Daily reset: all plants are reset at the start of each new day.
Notifications: a daily reminder at 12:00 AM to water your plants.
Dark mode: the whole app is forced to a black dark theme.
